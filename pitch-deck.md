## **Slide 2: The Problem**
*   **Headline:** The Extension Crisis
*   **Key Points:**
    *   **1:10,000 ratio:** The devastating gap between extension workers and farmers in Nigeria.
    *   **Information Asymmetry:** Millions of farmers lack access to modern research.
    *   **Silent Loss:** Prevents trillions in potential yield due to untreated pests and poor soil management.
*   **🖼️ Visual Suggestion:** A split-screen graphic. On the left, a lonely extension worker at a desk; on the right, a vast map of Nigeria with thousands of red dots representing underserved farms.

## **Slide 3: The Opportunity**

*   **🖼️ Visual Suggestion:** An "explosion" graphic showing a library of books turning into digitial data particles flowing into a mobile phone.

    *   An AI-powered multimodal assistant.

## **Slide 5: How It Works: Multi-Modal Vision**
*   **Headline:** Instant Biological Field Scans
*   **Key Points:**
    *   Powered by **Claude 3.7 Sonnet**.
    *   Farmers upload a single photo of a sick leaf/crop.
    *   The AI "sees" physical symptoms (lesions, patterns, color) with professional accuracy.
*   **🖼️ Visual Suggestion:** An image of a leaf with a digital "scanning" line passing over it, highlighting specific spots where the AI has identified symptoms.
